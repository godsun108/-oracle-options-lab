# Prospective ETF paper league

Season `etf-open-paper-2026-10-v1` groups the existing fixed strategies into six
market teams: SPY, QQQ, IWM, SMH, TLT and GLD. These are automated strategy groups,
not six independent AI agents, and these ETFs do not represent all markets.

Rules are registered before the first signal session, October 15, 2026. The
first hypothetical entry is October 16. Each team starts with $10,000 of
hypothetical capital and allocates equal daily weights to the three unchanged
long/cash strategies. Each invested sleeve pays an assumed 25 basis points per
side for each daily round trip. Cash earns zero. No borrowing or leverage.

The season covers 60 consecutive, identical entry/exit windows. Reports expose
progress and missing eligible windows. Returns, drawdown, cost-free market
comparison, and descriptive ordering appear only for complete teams after the
fixed window. A missing observation is never a zero return or a cash decision.
Incomplete teams remain visible and do not prevent complete teams from reporting.
All six losing money still produces an ordering, not a profitable winner.

Individual candidate research review remains separate and can advance while
other teams are blocked. Neither review nor league placement enables live orders.
The 18-hypothesis family remains intact. Sixty sessions is a research convention,
not proof of significance; selection, repeated testing, regimes, actual execution
and portfolio risk still require independent review.

## Evidence and fair experimentation

The scorer audits the ledger's hashes, registration timing, source checkpoints,
and return arithmetic. It recalculates league returns from audited opening prices,
not team-supplied profit totals. Reports bind to ledger and rule hashes. The rules
pin signal, ledger and calendar implementation fingerprints; changing those files
halts season scoring until an explicit reviewed resolution. Never silently update
the fingerprints to preserve a favorable season. A legitimate correction requires
documenting its effect, preserving the old evidence and registering a new season.

Creative experiments belong in separate branches and new prospective strategy
versions. Record code, parameters, data requirements, costs and evaluation window
before collecting their outcomes. Preserve failed experiments and expand the
testing family; do not replace losers, reuse held-out data as fresh evidence, or
alter existing signal identities. This version deliberately provides no autonomous
code mutation or strategy admission mechanism.

GitHub workflow logs, source artifacts and cross-run ledger continuity supply the
existing evidence trail. Hashes cannot prevent a privileged party rewriting code
and evidence together, establish source-provider authenticity, or prove fills.
Artifacts have finite retention; this is not permanent external attestation.
Long-lived immutable evidence storage remains unfinished. No new paid services,
live orders, or autonomous profitability certification are introduced.
