Trading playbook

Use this as a repeatable process. No indicator or flow signal guarantees an outcome.

Market direction

Study SPY, QQQ, IWM, and DIA. Compare price with 20- and 50-day averages, previous highs/lows, and the broader trend. Do not force a trade in a mixed market.

VWAP & volume

VWAP is a useful intraday reference. Compare current volume with normal activity. A breakout without participation may fail. Markets Now's initial scanner does not yet calculate intraday VWAP.

RSI & MACD

RSI helps describe momentum and potential overbought/oversold conditions; MACD helps study momentum shifts. Neither is a standalone entry signal. RSI 14 is displayed when enough daily bars are available.

Support & resistance

Mark prior-day high/low, premarket high/low, prior close, and major price zones. Wait for a breakout and a convincing retest rather than chasing a large candle.

Options liquidity

Check bid/ask width, displayed size, volume, and open interest. A cheap premium is not automatically a bargain. Avoid contracts with poor liquidity or wide spreads.

The Greeks

Delta: price sensitivity. Gamma: delta's rate of change. Theta: time decay. Vega: implied-volatility sensitivity. Rho: interest-rate sensitivity. Buyers can lose premium as time passes.

Implied volatility

High IV can make options expensive. Earnings and major announcements can cause IV crush: the option can lose value even when the underlying moves in your direction. Check IV rank/percentile where available.

VIX & macro calendar

Watch VIX, Fed decisions, CPI, PCE, payrolls, unemployment, and GDP. Event risk can change spreads, volatility, and option prices quickly.

Calls, puts & inverse ETFs

Calls can express bullish views; puts can express bearish views. Inverse ETFs target opposite daily index returns and can diverge over longer periods because of daily resets. Options on leveraged ETFs add risk.

Opening range breakout

Mark the first 15–30 minutes' high and low. Watch for a break with volume and a successful retest. Paper trade it before risking capital.

Unusual options activity

Unusual volume or large prints can be hedges, spreads, or closing trades. Volume above open interest is a clue to investigate, not proof of insider knowledge or direction.

Expected move & GEX

Expected move is inferred from option prices; gamma exposure estimates how dealer hedging may influence price behavior. Both require careful sourcing and interpretation.

Exit before entry

Write down invalidation, maximum loss, profit-taking plan, and position size before entering. Stops on options may slip or fail to execute at the intended price.

Never average down blindly

Adding to a losing option increases exposure. Only add when the original thesis and total risk budget still justify it. Do not chase losses or trade from FOMO.

Expiration discipline

Start by studying 30–60 DTE contracts. Avoid 0DTE while learning. Time decay accelerates near expiration, and short-dated options can lose their entire premium.

Paper trade & review

Log at least 30–50 paper trades. Compare results after fees and realistic bid/ask execution. Review expectancy, drawdown, win rate, and rule adherence—not just the biggest winner.
